What to click. The Cell type layer opens on the 10 major types. Click the ▸ chevron on a row to expand that major into its subtypes and leave the others collapsed — so you can show TREM2⁺ macrophages at subtype level beside Fibroblast at major level, in one view. Expand all / Collapse all do every row at once. The dropdown also holds Activation (just the two populations of interest) and Transfer confidence. Each legend row has a colour picker — your choices persist in the browser — and a genes button listing that label’s top 20 marker genes; click a gene to colour cells by its expression. The ◎ button rings those cells, or paste cell IDs into the highlight box.

Read these caveats before quoting anything.

1. This panel resolves subtypes far less well than the 6K does. Of the 1,000 probes, 992 are human genes and 923 of those are in the reference. But the median reference subtype keeps only 26% of its marker list on this panel, against 68% on the 6K; 19 of 57 subtypes keep fewer than 20 markers and one keeps 2. A subtype call here is a best match among 60 options on a thin gene set, not a confident identification. The per-subtype table is in A00_verify_setup/02_verify/subtype_marker_coverage.csv.

2. The immediate-early programme is essentially absent. EGR1, EGR2, NR4A1/2/3, KDM6B, FOSB and TRAC are all off-panel; only FOS, IER3, ATF3 and DUSP1 survive. Both populations on the Activation layer are defined in the reference by exactly that programme: PTB antigen-activated T keeps 10 of its 40 reference markers here and early-response macrophages 66 of 437. Treat that layer as a pointer for inspection, not as a count.

3. Between-sample composition is confounded with segmentation. PTB 21.1 and PTB 24.3 were segmented with seg_2026; PTB 22.1, PTB 22.3, PTB 24.1 and PTB 24.2 with seg_202606. Median library size also ranges 2x across samples (114 to 217 counts). A composition difference between samples is not automatically biology. PTB 22.1 and PTB 22.3 are two tissue blocks from one slide, split at a measured 2.19 mm void in y.

4. Immunofluorescence is not uniform. CD3 exists on five of the six samples but not on PTB 21.1, which carries CD68 instead. CD45, PanCK, Membrane and DAPI are on all six. Any CD3-based statement excludes PTB 21.1.

5. The QC floor is deliberately low. Cells were kept at ≥20 counts (95.1% retained), chosen because every higher floor preferentially deletes T cells — at 100 counts the deleted cells are up to 2.4x more T-enriched than those kept, and PTB 21.1 would lose 45% of its cells. The cost is that sparse cells are admitted; they are flagged on the Transfer confidence layer rather than deleted, so filter there rather than assuming every cell is well measured.

6. Proliferating is a single-subtype label that absorbs T cells in this project’s earlier runs, so treat it with suspicion rather than as a proliferation measure. The panel also carries eight bacterial 16S/23S probes (M. tuberculosis/bovis, M. leprae, C. acnes, pan-bacterial) which are excluded from the RNA assay and from the transfer; they are at background level and are not shown here.
